Learning guide · no estimates

A holding weight tells you what you own. A company report explains its business. A beta measures how its share price moved with oil. You need all three to investigate hidden exposure.

Start with dated holdings, inspect a company’s original report, then compare that business explanation with a historical estimate and its uncertainty. A sector label alone cannot answer the question.

Notes. Figures are historical associations from weekly data (19 Nov 2021 to 13 Nov 2026, 261 weeks, CAD returns), not forecasts or causal estimates. Scenarios assume the historical beta holds and leave out every other source of risk. Preview numbers come from saved outputs of compute/run_stats.ipynb and have not been independently verified. Portfolios are fictional. Nothing here is investment advice.

Method

Weekly CAD log returns for each of the data contract’s 14 core tickers (nine ETFs, five stocks) are regressed on weekly Brent log returns (weeks end Friday, 19 Nov 2021 to 13 Nov 2026, 261 weeks). Ordinary least squares with a constant; Newey–West standard errors with four lags. USD tickers are converted to CAD with the weekly USD/CAD change.

Why only these 14 tickers

They are the MVP tickers in docs/data_contract.md: the only ones with saved results the sample portfolios can use. Anything else has no numbers here, so research, the watchlist and the portfolio builder only offer these.

The 95% range

Beta ± 1.96 standard errors. If it crosses zero, the sample doesn’t settle the direction. It’s a range for the coefficient, not for future returns.

Documents and attribution

The Evidence Desk reads an independent local research snapshot: five confirmed XIU holdings and their source passages. Its holdings weights describe composition, not contributions to oil beta. The market view below uses separate notebook or backend statistics.

Shock scenarios

Only −30%, −15%, +15% and +30% Brent shocks exist. Impact = 100 × (exp(β × ln(1 + shock/100)) − 1). The range transforms the coefficient bounds and ignores everything else that moves returns.

Portfolios

Robert, Amira and Mateo are fictional personas from the data contract. Their local saved results use the earlier weighted-log-return method and are labeled legacy previews. The current backend regresses log(1 + Σ(weight × (exp(asset log return) − 1))) on Brent. A custom portfolio shows only a weighted-beta proxy, not an exact portfolio beta or a confidence interval. Updated portfolio results require fresh backend outputs.

What’s local

Preview numbers are rounded outputs saved in compute/run_stats.ipynb. Ticker scenarios are recomputed from them with the formula above; persona scenarios, alternatives and the bond duration check are saved notebook outputs. Documentary research loads separately from evidence-preview.json, with its original verification limits. No rolling history is supplied, and nothing here checks the live state of AWS. Your profile and portfolios stay in this browser; there are no accounts.
