About RIPPLE AI

See what really moved your portfolio, and what protected it

RIPPLE is a research agent. Ask about your portfolio in plain words: it measures how interest rates, stocks and oil moved your holdings over 2021–2026, reads company and central-bank sources, and answers with a verdict you can check step by step.

01

Test what you believe

“My bonds protect me.” “Gold is a hedge.” Say it, and RIPPLE runs the matching test on your holdings, then answers with a verdict: REAL, MYTH, WORKING, BROKEN, MINOR…

02

Three risks, one picture

Interest rates, stocks and oil are measured separately, so you see which one actually drove your weekly moves instead of guessing.

03

Numbers you can trace

Every figure is tagged measured or source, and “How RIPPLE reasoned” lists each tool the agent called and what it found.

04

Honest by design

No buy or sell advice, no forecasts. A guardrail checks every answer, and history is described as history: 2021–2026, past behaviour, not a prediction.

What a shock looks like

A shock you could feel at the pump

Canadian gasoline peaked at 207.2¢ a litre in Jun 2022. Shocks like this hit oil, interest rates and stock markets at the same time.

RIPPLE measures how each of those moved your holdings, then checks whether your shields (bonds, gold, the US dollar) actually cushioned the worst weeks. Latest reading: 184.3¢.
