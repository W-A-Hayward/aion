### CPKC-2025-02 — fuel_surcharge_pass_through

PDF **23**, page imprimée **32** — Form 10-K — Fuel Cost Adjustment Program

**Citation originale :**

> The short-term volatility in fuel prices may adversely or positively impact revenues. The Company employs a fuel cost adjustment program designed to
> respond to fluctuations in fuel prices and help reduce volatility to changing fuel prices. Fuel surcharge revenues are earned on individual shipments and
> are based primarily on the price of On-Highway Diesel in Canada and the U.S. and the public fuel price for Petróleos Mexicanos ("PEMEX") TAR Irapuato
> in Mexico. As such, fuel surcharge revenues are a function of freight volumes and fuel prices. Fuel surcharge revenues accounted for approximately 10%
> of the Company's Freight revenues in 2025. The Company is also subject to carbon taxation systems and levies in some jurisdictions in which it operates,
> the costs of which are passed on to the shipper. As such, fuel surcharge revenue includes recoveries of carbon taxes and levies.

**Fait divulgué :** Surcharges fondées surtout sur On-Highway Diesel Canada/USA et prix PEMEX Mexique ; environ 10 % des revenus de fret 2025.

**Notre interprétation :** La facturation peut amortir une partie du choc de coût carburant.

**Limites :** Pas de garantie de compensation intégrale ; taxes incluses ; références diesel, pas Brent.

### CPKC-2025-03 — fuel_surcharge_recovery_timing

PDF **24**, page imprimée **33** — Form 10-K — Fuel Cost Adjustment Program (continued)

**Citation originale :**

> Freight revenues included fuel surcharge revenues of $1,483 million in 2025, a decrease of $168 million, or 10%, from $1,651 million in the same period

**Notre interprétation :** Le calendrier des recouvrements peut laisser une exposition temporaire.

**Limites :** Aucun délai de deux mois indiqué : ne pas transférer celui de CNR. Pas d’effet boursier inféré.
